About PathWars

PathWars is a free, browser-based version of the classic board game Quoridor. No download, no signup — open the page and play against real people, a friend by room code, or the computer.

▶ Play now

What this site is

PathWars is an online strategy game for 2 or 4 players. On your turn you either move your pawn one square or place a wall. Walls cannot be jumped over, only walked around — and the game never lets you block an opponent off completely. The first to reach the goal wins.

Three tables are open around the clock:

  • Duel 9×9 — the classic. Two players start on opposite sides with 10 walls each.
  • Race 9×13 — both start at the bottom and race to the top with 10 or 15 walls.
  • Four at a table 11×11 — four players race for the golden cell in the centre, 7 walls each.

A match takes two to five minutes. Full details are in the Rules; practical answers (account, friend code, installing on a phone, deleting an account) are in Help.

Who runs it

PathWars is built and operated by a small independent team. The publisher of this website is PathWars, reachable at contact@pathwars.online and on Telegram at PathWarsChannel. Advertising enquiries: ads@pathwars.online.

We answer every message, usually within a few days. The fastest way to reach us is Telegram; for privacy or account matters, email is better.

Why it is free

The game is free for everyone and has no paid advantages — supporting the project never buys extra walls, points or rank. The servers are paid for by advertising: ad units appear in the menus only, never during a match, and the video behind “Support PathWars” opens only if you tap it.
