How ratings work

After a match the game offers one question that takes a single tap: one to five stars. Words are optional — most people leave stars only, and that is a complete answer. One to three stars become a private note to us; four and five stars are offered a place on this public page. To keep the board honest, rating unlocks with an account or after ten matches played.

What gets published

Good and bad appear here unedited, in the player's own words and language. Where we have an answer — a fixed bug, a clarified rule — it sits under the review. Likes on reviews come from players too. Nothing here is bought, and nothing is written by us.

About the game

New to Quoridor? Start with the Rules (move or wall, jumps, why a full block is refused, Duel 9×9, Race 9×13 and the four-player 11×11 table), then Help for friend codes and installing on a phone, and About PathWars for who runs the game and how to reach us.
